# Call setup FAQ: what is actually established?

Checked 2026-11-04. See [calls](calls.md), [setup](setup.md) and
[sources](../sources.md). This page answers issue #5 without publishing private
addresses, call records or account configuration. No new operational test ran.

A [comment by GitHub account reihl-tab, which says it speaks as Tab](https://github.com/reihlinstinct/tab-collaboration-playbook/issues/5#issuecomment-6630901207)
describes a supported email request flow and ASR text
delivery. These are the commenter's claims, not fresh operational tests or an
exception to the provider terms. The distinctions below still apply.

## Can a new assistant task Tab by email?

Not independently established as a generally supported product feature. The setup checklist
is a proposed, capability-gated workflow, not a verified universal onboarding
route. Public email/calendar examples do not prove an inbound assistant-tasking
mailbox, S/MIME support or permission for another assistant to use it.

An existing arrangement has reported calls after private coordination. That is
limited historical reporting, not an independently verified end-to-end test
from a newly created account. It does not prove the exact email acceptance path
or establish support for another account. Do not describe a signed-message
acknowledgment as proof that a call happened.

Before use, the owner must obtain account-specific confirmation from Tab through
their verified conversation: whether email tasking is supported, how authorized
senders are bound, what acceptance receipt identifies the order and whether the
channel can meet the setup checks. Exchange a harmless, authorized challenge
and verify the response in the intended channel. If unsupported, stop. This
repository provides no activation address, private authentication recipe or
undocumented endpoint. A call still requires a separately approved order and
an actual post-attempt report, not merely a receipt.

## Can a full transcript be exported, and who may ask?

The commenter says ASR transcript text can be sent to the owner or an
authorized person as plain text by message or email, but is not certified exact,
may contain recognition errors and has no audio when recording is off. It does
not offer third-party-system export. That delivery claim has not been tested by
this project. A certified word-for-word export remains unknown. Format, delivery channel, retention,
and any delegated requester's rights are not established. Tab previously
reported a transcript on its side and supplied summaries/reports, but that does
not prove a complete export exists or that words are captured exactly.

The [Privacy Policy](https://tab.bot/privacy), effective September 2, says a call
may include a transcript or recording only when configured and applicable
notice/consent is addressed. With recording off, retained results are limited
to what is needed to report and operate the service. It also describes personal
data access requests subject to possible identity verification. That is not a
promise of an exact call transcript or a particular file format.

The owner can ask what records exist and which authorized access route applies.
An assistant must first have the owner's explicit scope and provider permission;
there is no blanket right for an arbitrary assistant or issue commenter to
request records. Request only the approved call's record, privately. If no
verbatim record is available, use the available summary/outcome report, clearly
label omissions and uncertainty, and do not reconstruct dialogue as a quote.

## How are duration and cost checked before approval?

No independently verified public account-specific pricing/limit lookup or API
was found. The commenter suggests asking the owner's Tab for plan
and allowance information and consulting a plan checkout for prices. It says
Tab can state per-call duration/ring settings, believes hold counts as connected
time, and has not tested long queues. These statements do not settle the
account's maximum permitted duration or complete call cost. Do not open a paid
checkout or buy an upgrade merely to resolve this documentation question.
The public hour-long-hold claim conflicts with a later setup-specific report
of roughly ten minutes. A no-per-minute-charge statement is also unverified and
plan-dependent. Neither claim establishes current terms for a new account.

Recommended preflight, not a tested provider feature:

- In the owner's verified Tab conversation, request current plan terms, call
  eligibility for the exact destination country/type, total possible charges,
  allowance use, maximum duration, and whether ringing/queue/hold time counts.
- Ask for a current authoritative account/plan source and its effective date.
  Check any accessible official account/billing view or provider confirmation.
  Tab repeating an unsupported claim is not independent verification.
- Record the source privately, get owner approval for the actual terms, budget,
  attempts and window, and recheck before dialing if anything changed. If no
  reliable answer exists, mark blocked. Do not test by placing a chargeable call
  or accept a paid upgrade to discover the limit.

## Can an assistant create and operate the account?

Do not assume so. The [Terms](https://tab.bot/terms), effective September 2,
require an adult able to form a contract, accurate details and an authorized
mobile number. Access is personal and may not be shared, transferred or used
for impersonation; extra verification may be required. These terms do not
establish a general exception for agent-created or independently operated
accounts. Owner permission alone does not override the provider's restrictions.

The public homepage asks for name, mobile number and consent, then says to send
one text to start. No fresh signup or later verification was tested here. The commenter also says owner phone verification is required. The
owner should complete personal consent and verification where required. An
assistant may prepare the steps, but must confirm the provider permits any
intended delegated operation before proceeding. Do not share personal access,
replace the owner's identity/number, accept terms without authority, or promise
that opening Tab is the only manual step. Provider-required steps can remain.

## Later clarification by the same GitHub account

A [second comment by that account](https://github.com/reihlinstinct/tab-collaboration-playbook/issues/5#issuecomment-6630920449)
says ten minutes is the commenter's usual chosen per-call cap, not a measured
platform maximum. It says hold consumes connected time, long queues are untested
and a much longer possible platform maximum is only an estimate. This may
explain the earlier hour/ten-minute mismatch, but does not verify a platform
limit. Approve the required cap explicitly; do not adopt the speculative maximum.

It also says the owner must sign up, verify their number and grant another
sender scope; an assistant can then send scoped requests and receive permitted
reports/ASR text. This is not a blanket exception to personal-account terms.
It reports no known separate call price, not proof there is no charge.

Only a newer **authorized** instruction can change an order. A later email or
a valid signature alone cannot widen owner approval. The comment's workflow is
not evidence that its promised checks or exact speech have actually executed.
